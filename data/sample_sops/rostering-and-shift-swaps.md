# Rostering and Shift Swaps (Sample)

> **SAMPLE DATA.** This is a fictional, generic policy document written to demo the chatbot. It is not the policy of any real restaurant. Always follow your own workplace policies and Australian food-safety law.

## Roster release
The weekly roster is released by Thursday for the following Monday to Sunday. Shifts are split into open, lunch and close slots.

## Availability
Update your availability in the rostering app at least two weeks ahead. Changes made later than that may not be picked up until the next roster.

## Swapping a shift
You may swap a shift with another team member if they are trained for that slot. A close shift can only be swapped with someone who is close-trained. Both people must agree, and the swap must be approved by a shift manager before it counts. Until it is approved, the original person is still responsible for the shift.

## Sick leave and no-shows
If you are sick, call the store (do not just text) at least two hours before your shift starts. Not turning up without telling anyone is treated as a serious issue.
