# Closing Procedure (Sample)

> **SAMPLE DATA.** This is a fictional, generic policy document written to demo the chatbot. It is not the policy of any real restaurant. Always follow your own workplace policies and Australian food-safety law.

## Who can close
Only team members who have completed closing training and are marked as close-trained on the roster may be rostered on a close shift. Every close needs at least one shift manager and at least one close-trained crew member. A new starter can shadow a close but cannot be counted as a closer.

## Front of house
Lock the front door at the advertised closing time. Serve customers already inside, then stack chairs, wipe tables and sweep and mop the dining floor. Turn off the menu screens and music.

## Kitchen
Turn off and clean the grill, fryers and benches. Filter or change fryer oil according to the oil schedule. Label, date and cover all prepped food before moving it to the cool room. Throw away any food past its use-by time.

## Cash and security
The shift manager counts the till twice with a second team member as witness and records the totals in the closing log. Cash goes in the safe. Set the alarm, check that the back door is locked, and leave the building together. No one leaves alone after a close.
