# Breaks and Incident Reporting (Sample)

> **SAMPLE DATA.** This is a fictional, generic policy document written to demo the chatbot. It is not the policy of any real restaurant. Always follow your own workplace policies and Australian food-safety law.

## Breaks
Your break entitlement depends on your shift length and your award or agreement. Check with the shift manager at the start of the shift when your break is planned. Breaks are taken away from the service area.

## Injuries
Report every injury, burn or cut to the shift manager straight away, however small. Use the first-aid kit in the office and record the incident in the incident log before the end of the shift.

## Customer complaints
Listen, apologise and get the shift manager. Do not argue with the customer. The shift manager decides on any refund or replacement and records the complaint.

## Emergencies
In an emergency call 000 first, then tell the shift manager. Know where the fire exits, fire blanket and extinguishers are.
