# Uniform and Presentation (Sample)

> **SAMPLE DATA.** This is a fictional, generic policy document written to demo the chatbot. It is not the policy of any real restaurant. Always follow your own workplace policies and Australian food-safety law.

## Uniform
Wear the supplied shirt and cap or hair net, black pants, and closed-toe, non-slip black shoes. Aprons are provided in the store and must not be worn outside.

## Hair, jewellery and nails
Long hair must be tied back and covered. Only a plain band ring and small stud earrings are allowed. Nails must be short and clean, with no nail polish or false nails in the kitchen.

## Phones
Phones stay in your locker or bag during the shift and can be used on breaks.
