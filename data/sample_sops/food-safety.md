# Food Safety Basics (Sample)

> **SAMPLE DATA.** This is a fictional, generic policy document written to demo the chatbot. It is not the policy of any real restaurant. Always follow your own workplace policies and Australian food-safety law.

## Temperatures
Keep cold food at 5°C or below and hot food at 60°C or above. Check and record the cool room and freezer temperatures at opening, mid-shift and closing. The freezer should be at -15°C or colder. If the cool room reads above 5°C, tell the shift manager straight away.

## The 2-hour / 4-hour rule
Ready-to-eat food that has been between 5°C and 60°C for less than 2 hours can be used or refrigerated. Between 2 and 4 hours it must be used straight away. After 4 hours it must be thrown away.

## Allergens
Treat every allergy question seriously. Check the allergen matrix before answering, never guess, and tell the shift manager. Use clean utensils and a clean board for allergy orders.

## Hand washing
Wash hands for at least 20 seconds at the start of a shift, after breaks, after handling raw meat, after touching your face or phone, and after taking out rubbish.
